# Role dashboards and client flows

One unified Next.js app with authorized role route layouts;Flutter consumes the same API. Platform,School Admin,Principal,Teacher,Student andParent screens are distinct. URL/layout visibility is not backend authorization.

Public registration/activation/context selection;SA admissions/accounts/guardian links/configuration/approvals/fees/subscription;Principal academics/profile admissions/review/substitutes/limited fees;Teacher assigned attendance/homework/marks/notices/leave;Student own history/homework Done;Parent only verified linked-child history and fees.

Demo banner:30days remaining,upload unavailable,shared sample action;non-file CRUD remains available. Free Live shows5MB/PDF,activePDF/50,daily/10,total/500MB,80%warning and most restrictive denial. Paid shows effective policy version/limits. Expiry preserves authorized reads and narrows writes;family never sees commercial subscription details. At16:00display school timezone/correction request action. No student upload controls,chat or family leave notifications.

Accessible labels,keyboard/focus/large touch targets,slow-network retry,empty/loading/error/version-conflict states. Clear tenant/child caches on switch/revocation. No durable offline child-data/file queue in initial baseline. English UI with Bengali/Hindi translation structure.
